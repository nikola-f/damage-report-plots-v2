Privacy Policy

Damage Report Plots · Last updated: August 10, 2026

Damage Report Plots ("the app", "we") is a client-side web application that reads your "Ingress Damage Report" emails from Gmail, extracts the portal locations they describe, records them in a Google Sheet that the app creates in your own Google Drive, and lets you copy the resulting list to your clipboard for use in an IITC map plugin.

The app has no backend server. All processing happens entirely inside your browser. Your Gmail data, the access token, and everything derived from them are never sent to, stored on, or logged by any server operated by us.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, you grant the app the following OAuth scopes. Access lasts only for the ~1-hour lifetime of a single access token; the app requests no refresh token.

  • gmail.readonly — read-only access to Gmail, used only to find and parse the HTML body of "Ingress Damage Report" emails so it can extract portal names and coordinates. The app reads no other messages beyond what this purpose requires and never modifies, sends, or deletes email.
  • drive.file — access limited to the single spreadsheet the app itself creates. It cannot see any of your other Drive files. This lets the app find and reuse the same spreadsheet across devices.
  • email and profile — your basic Google account identity (name, email address, profile picture), used to show who is signed in.

How your data is used and stored

  • Gmail message contents are parsed in your browser and immediately reduced to portal records (location, count, timestamp). Raw email content is not retained after processing.
  • The extracted portal records are written to a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive, which remains under your control. You can view, edit, or delete it at any time.
  • The clipboard copy you trigger stays on your device; the app does not transmit it anywhere.
  • We keep no copy of any of this data. There is no server-side database, storage, or logging of your Google user data.

Limited Use disclosure

Damage Report Plots' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, data obtained through Gmail restricted scopes is used solely to provide and improve the app's user-facing feature described above, is processed only on your device, is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide that feature (writing to your own Google Sheet), is never used for advertising, and is never sold. No humans read your data, except where required for security, to comply with applicable law, or with your explicit consent.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your Google user data with any third party. The app communicates only with Google's own APIs (Gmail, Drive, Sheets, and Google Identity Services) directly from your browser.

Security

Sign-in uses the Google Identity Services token model with PKCE and no client secret. The access token is held in memory for the duration of your session only and is discarded when the token expires or you sign out. Because there is no server, there is no server-side attack surface for your Google data.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is not directed to children under the age required to hold a Google account in their jurisdiction and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
